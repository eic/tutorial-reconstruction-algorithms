---
title: Setup
---

To follow along with this tutorial you will need:

1. A GitHub account ([sign up here](https://github.com/signup)).
2. A working `eic-shell` environment. If you do not have one yet, first complete the
   [Setting Up Your EIC Environment](https://eic.github.io/tutorial-setting-up-environment/)
   tutorial.
3. A local checkout of [EICrecon](https://github.com/eic/EICrecon) that you can build from inside
   `eic-shell`. Building EICrecon from source can take a while, so do this in advance of the
   session. Build instructions are in the EICrecon
   [README](https://github.com/eic/EICrecon#readme).

You should also be comfortable reading and writing C++.
